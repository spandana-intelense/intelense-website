Frequent Questions.

Everything you need to know about Intelense solutions and how they integrate into your current workflow.

What is Edge Intelligence?

Edge intelligence refers to processing data on the device itself (at the 'edge') rather than sending all data to a centralized cloud. This results in faster response times and lower bandwidth costs.

Is my data secure with Intelense?

Absolutely. We prioritize data privacy and security, with end-to-end encryption and the option for completely offline processing for sensitive environments.
