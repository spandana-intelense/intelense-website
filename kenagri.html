What KenAgri Does

Smart Farming, From Soil to Sky

KenAgri leverages IoT sensors, drone imagery, and computer vision to deliver real-time insights that improve crop health, resource efficiency, and farm productivity.

Collect Data

Integrate sensors, satellite imagery, drones, and field inputs.

Analyze AI

Process data using crop-specific and region-aware AI models.

Deliver Insights

Recommendations for irrigation, nutrition, and protection.

Improve

Continuously refine insights with historical and real-time data.
